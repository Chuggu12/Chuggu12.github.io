Behavioural Insights from WHO Road Safety Campaign

POWER BI | EXCEL

An advanced Power BI dashboard to analyse ten years of qualitative and behavioural data from WHO’s Road Safety Campaign conducted across 9 major Mexican cities. The project involved studying household- and community-level behavioural responses, perception shifts, and campaign effectiveness over time. Using Power BI’s data-modelling and interactive-visualisation capabilities, I transformed raw qualitative inputs into structured behavioural indicators—attitude shifts, risk perceptions, compliance patterns, and demographic-based differences. Advanced Time Series Simulation & ARIMA Forecasting

STATA | PYTHON

Conducted extensive time-series simulations, generating between 10 and 10,000 realizations of Random Walk, AR(1), AR(2), MA(1), and MA(2) processes to analyse convergence behaviour, oscillation patterns, and long-run variance stability. To isolate the stochastic component of each series, I applied detrending and differencing transformations and validated stationarity using DF, ADF, and KPSS testing frameworks. I further compared linear, quadratic, and cubic deterministic trend models using AIC/BIC, empirically establishing the linear trend as the optimal specification. Using these diagnostics, I estimated multiple ARIMA configurations—(0,1,1), (1,1,0), (1,1,1), (2,1,0), and (0,1,2)—and selected the best-fit model through information criteria and residual analysis. The project also included a complete forecasting pipeline, incorporating PACF/ACF interpretation, residual validation, and visualisation of out-of-sample predictions.

WOLFRAM MATHEMATICA

  • Implemented AM mathematically using y(t) = [A + M·cos(ωmt + ϕ)]·sin(ωct), focusing on the interaction between baseband and high-frequency carriers.

Closing loopholes: Real-time digital media monitoring using AI-tools to counter digital tobacco marketing

Published in Tobacco Induced Diseases (JCI: Q2, IF: 2.8) under special issue of 2025

  • In this study, I worked as part of the core research team that developed and deployed a real-time AI-based digital media monitoring system to detect covert tobacco marketing across India, Indonesia, Mexico, and cross-border platforms. The system uncovered over 41,000 instances of policy-evading digital advertising, despite stringent national restrictions.
  • My role focused on deep behavioural analysis of these violations—examining marketing tactics, message framings, platform strategies, and third-party retailer behaviour used to exploit legal loopholes across countries. I analysed how companies and retailers used surrogate branding, cultural/event framing, lifestyle messaging, and algorithmic amplification to target vulnerable demographics, especially youth.
  • Overall, the research demonstrates how AI-driven surveillance can transform tobacco control by replacing slow, manual monitoring with continuous, evidence-based detection of marketing violations, enabling faster enforcement and stronger policy action.
